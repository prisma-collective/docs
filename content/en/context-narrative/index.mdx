> The purpose of prisma is to align human economy with increasing orders of ecological health. 

That requires working in new ways, which may not be immediately intuitive to everyone. It may even be the case that alternate futures come from imagination into reality. In order to be able to work on that at the collective level, as a stepping stone toward viable planetary futures, prisma aims to create data-backed examples of what those new beginnings look like. We might even advocate for the crucial need to learn from experimenting with those new beginnings. Put simply, that means being able to ask "What worked, what didn't, and why?" Together, this starts to look like action and learning. 

In fact, prisma is not just aiming to create those kinds of data-backed examples itself, but is in actuality working on a distributed system to support the self-organising of those examples. Really, we are aiming to build capacity at exactly this stepping stone kind of place: experimentations in increasing orders of life-centric economy. 

That's why we refer to "events." They're not your usual kind of event. Instead, they are highly intentional co-created spaces built with the purpose of evidencing and publishing this kind of activity. They're innately experimental, because the aim is to generate a systemic shift. No one knows exactly what the deciding factor will be to bring that about, since there are an immeasurable number of dimensions that need to align in just the right way at just the right moment. We call those events action-learning journeys. 

> The purpose of action-learning journeys are to evidence systems-change as a step toward increasing orders of life-centric economy. 

Our approach to action-learning journeys, in fact, includes several months of inter-disciplinary and participatory design, with multiple levels of inter-related stakeholders. Note, not just multiple stakeholders, but multiple *levels* of stakeholders. That means it's not just a group of people wearing different hats, but that those hats mean they're participating from an entirely distinct order of system dynamics. It's like a bird and a tree. They move wholly differently, and have very different lives and perspectives, but both are part of the same ecosystem, which has multiple levels. There is also almost certainly an ant in that very same ecosystem, somewhere. 

Those spaces, with that aim, are a fascinating inter-play of many interrelated conditions that contribute to giving rise to those kinds of systemic shifts. That's why, as a second offering to this purpose, alongside events-organising, we have been developing the means for publishing. 

---

The explanation so far draws from and attempts to balance several relatively niche domains from the ecological transition space, from Narrative Intervention (Culture Hack Labs), to Regenerative Design (Regenesis Institute), to Systematics (Bohm and Bennett), and even all the way back to an intuition-led scientist from the 1800s working from a distinct mode of observation as an enabler for apprehending of the developmental nature of biology (Goethe). These lineages have been trying to communicate what sometimes can be overlooked: the wholeness of nature. So, before transitioning to publishing, please note that an overwhelming depth has been omitted for the sake of brevity. 

---

Publishing is where things get technical. Events organising is social, and publishing is technical. That's why the two go hand-in-hand, forming an inter-disciplinary bond that is absolutely essential for the wholeness of the capability of prisma as an organisation - and any other organisation that wants to carry this work forwards. 

> The purpose of publishing is to make visible the process of organising action-learning journeys, which evidence systems-change as a step toward increasing orders of life-centric economy.
