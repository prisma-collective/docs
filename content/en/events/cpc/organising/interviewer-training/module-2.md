# Module 2: Hypothesis-Testing Methodology, Bias Control & CASS Standards

## 1. Objective
Train interviewers across the **Buenos Aires Hub**, **Lagos Hub**, **Nairobi Hub**, and **Barcelona Benchmark** to test and falsify desk hypotheses (`H1-H7`, `H11-H12`) rather than pitch Cardano, enforce the non-Cardano independence quotas, and apply the four-tier Cardano Adoption Signal Standard (`CASS`).

## 2. Five Mandatory Bias Controls in the Field
1. **No Pitching or Leading Questions:** You are conducting empirical market, regulatory, and infrastructure due diligence, not selling blockchain adoption. Ask how the institution currently handles settlement, credentialing, or provenance, what operational costs or failures they experience, and whether a public ledger offers any advantage over existing domestic systems (`Transferencias 3.0`, `NIBSS NIP`, `M-Pesa`, `Bizum`, `NIMC NIN`, `QuarkID`, or `Decidim`).
2. **Non-Cardano Independence Quota (`>= 75%` Non-Cardano; `>= 2` per Region Floor):** Across each priority cluster (**Southern Cone**, **West Africa**, **East Africa**), at least 75% of interviewed counterparties must be independent non-Cardano institutions, and `Question Pack 7 (Cardano Ecosystem)` is capped at `<= 25%` of total engagements.
3. **Sub-National & Operational Grounding:** Pair capital-city regulatory and central bank perspectives with operational evidence from agricultural cooperatives, universities, trade guilds, licensed payment switches, and municipal innovation teams (`Lagos, Abuja, Northern Nigeria, and Accra` in West Africa; `Kenya, Rwanda, and Uganda` in East Africa; `Buenos Aires, Uruguay, and Paraguay` in the Southern Cone).
4. **Five-Tier Counterparty Honesty:** Classify every mapped counterparty strictly as `Interviewed`, `Warm Access`, `Referred`, `Desk-Identified`, or `Hypothetical`. An organisation transitions to `Interviewed` only after a completed interaction passes vault review and cross-hub verification.
5. **CASS Anti-Hype Enforcement:** Never classify a conference panel, social media post, or unfunded MOU above **`CASS Tier 4 (Weak Signal)`**. Only live production deployment with recurring transaction volume qualifies for `Tier 1`, and only signed, budgeted pilots with a named institutional owner qualify for `Tier 2`.
