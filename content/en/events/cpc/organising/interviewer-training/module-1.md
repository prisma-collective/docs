# Module 1: Research Ethics, Pre-Recording Consent and Vault Privacy

Every interview in Phase 3 starts with a clear agreement between the interviewer and the person taking part. Before any audio recorder or note-taking tool is switched on, the participant hears what the research is for, chooses how their words may be used, and receives a personal reference code they can use to change their mind later.

This module covers the rules that every Regional Hub Vault Reviewer across our three active [Communities of Place](/en/collaborators/communities-of-place/hub/hubs) (**Lagos Hub**, **Nairobi Hub** and **Buenos Aires Hub**) and every Local Project Team interviewer ([Hub Teams](/en/collaborators/communities-of-place/hub/hub%20team)) follows in the field.

---

## 1. The four consent choices and the separate AI question

Before starting a recording during [Enactment](/en/processes/enactment), the interviewer reads the short script from the [Local Stakeholder Consent Guide](/en/events/cpc/organising/enrolment/form-guidance/local-stakeholders) (which takes under a minute) and records the participant's choice:

1. **Private (the default).** The recording and notes stay inside the hub's encrypted vault so the interviewing team and the hub reviewer can check their own scoring. Nothing is quoted or shared outside the hub.
2. **Research (Cardano Product Committee and authorised researchers).** Personal names, contact details and any third parties mentioned in passing are removed. The edited anonymous transcript can be read only by the Cardano Product Committee and authorised project researchers through the vault's secure login. It is never published on the open web or written to an [AT Protocol](/en/processes/process-infrastructuring/publishing/atproto) repository. Original audio and video files never leave the vault.
3. **Anonymous public.** Personal names, contact details and third-party names are stripped out. Only approved anonymous quotes, structured findings and a cryptographic verification receipt are published to the team's repository.
4. **Public, named.** Statements approved by the participant are published alongside their name, role and organisation in the team's repository.
5. **A separate question on AI model training (No by default).** We ask separately whether the participant allows their anonymised words to be used to train external AI models in the future. The default is No. Transcription and name-redaction inside the hub vault run on the hub's own server and never send data to outside AI companies.

---

## 2. Three safeguards that apply to every interview

- **Private until reviewed.** Every new recording enters the hub vault set to Private. During the review session, the interviewer and the vault reviewer can keep the participant's chosen level or make it more private, but they cannot make a record more public without recording fresh permission from the participant.
- **A withdrawal reference number and a 12-month deletion limit.** Before the interview begins, the interviewer gives the participant a short reference code (for example, a code linking the region, city and sector) so that even someone who stays anonymous can ask for their interview to be changed or deleted later. A withdrawal request deletes the files from both the hub vault and the server backups. All raw audio, video and unredacted transcripts are deleted within 12 months of the end of the project.
- **Protecting other people mentioned in the room.** People often mention colleagues, clients or officials during an interview. Unless those third parties have also given permission, their names are removed along with disguised contact details such as spelled-out email addresses or spaced phone numbers. The vault review screen will not allow an anonymous record to be approved while any flagged name or contact detail remains unredacted.
