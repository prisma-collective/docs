# Module 3: Play Assembly, Respondent Screener & Interview Conduct

## 1. Pre-Interview Play Assembly (`Cluster x Use Case x Question Pack`)
Before scheduling or entering any call, the Local Team Interviewer and Hub Reviewer complete the **Assemble** block at the top of the chosen [Question Pack](/en/events/cpc/organising/question-packs):
- Select the **Regional Cluster** (**Southern Cone**, **West Africa**, **East Africa**, or **Iberia**) and **Active Play (`H1-H7`, `H11-H12`)**.
- Copy the **Falsifiable Desk Claims to Test** and **Falsification Trigger** from the [Use-Case Hypothesis Register](/en/events/cpc/executing/deliverables/use-case-hypothesis-register).
- Assign the participant's deterministic **`Respondent_ID` / Withdrawal Reference Number** (`e.g., SC-BA-PAY-01`, `WA-LOS-REG-02`, `EA-NRB-AGR-01`).

## 2. Executing the Standardized Interview Opening & Pre-Recording Consent
Begin every interaction with the [Interview Conduct Protocol](/en/events/cpc/executing/deliverables/research-design-document/interview-guide/conduct):
1. Thank the participant and confirm voluntary participation.
2. State the research purpose (regional adoption readiness and operational barrier assessment) and commissioning context (Cardano Product Committee `RFP 05`).
3. Read the 60-second [Consent Read-Aloud Script](/en/events/cpc/organising/enrolment/form-guidance/local-stakeholders) covering Choices `1. Private` (`Default`), `2. Research`, `3. Anonymous public`, and `4. Public, named`, plus the separate `AI Model Training` toggle (`Default: No`).
4. Issue the participant's **Withdrawal Reference Number** (`Respondent_ID`) and log their consent choice **before** starting the recording.

## 3. Probing Primary & Secondary Decision Gates
Keep the conversation anchored to the assembled play:
- Spend 70% of the call on the Question Pack's **Primary Gates** (`Regions`, `Use Cases`, `Counterparties`, `Regulation`, `Infrastructure Prerequisites`) and 30% on **Secondary Gates** (`Delivery Model`, `Adoption Signals`, `Cross-RFP Handoffs`).
- Before closing, ask for warm introductions (`Referred` counterparties) and explicit permission to name the referrer.
