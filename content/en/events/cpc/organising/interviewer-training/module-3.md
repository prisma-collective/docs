# Module 3: Preparing the Brief, Screening and Running the Interview

A good field interview starts before the call begins. During [Ground Potentialising](/en/processes/ground-potentialising) and [Enactment](/en/processes/enactment), the Local Team interviewer and the Hub Vault Reviewer put together a short preparation brief so that every question on the call tests a specific real-world claim.

---

## 1. Preparing the interview brief

Before confirming the meeting, the interviewer opens the relevant [Question Pack](/en/events/cpc/organising/question-packs) for the participant's role and fills in four details at the top:

- **The region and the specific workflow being tested.** For example, wholesale business-to-business currency corridors in Lagos and Accra, coffee and tea export traceability in Nairobi and Kampala, or dollar settlement escrow for cooperatives in Buenos Aires and Montevideo.
- **The desk claims and the falsification test.** Copy the specific cost, speed or legal assumptions from the [Use-Case Hypothesis Register](/en/events/cpc/executing/deliverables/use-case-hypothesis-register), along with the condition that would prove the hypothesis wrong in practice.
- **The local incumbent benchmark.** Note the exact local system the interviewee already uses so you can ask direct comparison questions during the call.
- **The participant's withdrawal reference number.** Create the short reference code the participant will keep in case they want to update or withdraw their contribution later.

---

## 2. Opening the call and recording consent

Every interview follows the steps in the [Conduct Guide](/en/events/cpc/executing/deliverables/research-design-document/interview-guide/conduct):

1. Thank the participant and confirm that taking part is voluntary.
2. Explain the purpose of the study: understanding real operational, regulatory and liquidity requirements for public-sector and emerging-market payment and credential workflows under the Cardano Product Committee research programme.
3. Read the 60-second [Consent Script](/en/events/cpc/organising/enrolment/form-guidance/local-stakeholders) covering the four visibility options (Private by default, Research, Anonymous public, and Public, named) and the separate question on AI model training (No by default).
4. Give the participant their withdrawal reference number and record their consent choice before switching on the recorder.

---

## 3. Running the conversation and asking for introductions

- **Focus on the primary decision gates first.** Spend roughly two-thirds of the conversation on the core operational questions for that participant's role: what their current system costs, what legal licences or compliance rules apply, and what liquidity or user-experience gaps stand in the way. Spend the final third on delivery models, concrete adoption evidence and links to other research areas.
- **Ask for warm introductions at the close.** Before ending the call, ask whether there is another regulator, cooperative, exporter or payment operator we should speak with, and check whether the participant is happy to be named when we make that introduction as part of [Ecosystem Weaving](/en/collaborators/core/business-development/alfred/ecosystem-weaving).
