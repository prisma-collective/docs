## Principles
The following principles shape the _how_ of project delivery.

1. Each hub is an autonomous node within its region. The hub must own its publication.
2. There are three hubs and one coordinator. This forms one trans-local cohort. This cohort will deliver these milestones in unison. No one gets left behind.
3. We aim for organisational excellence. We win and lose together.
