## Principles
The following principles shape the _how_ of project delivery.

1. Each hub is an autonomous node within its region. The hub must own its publication.
2. There are three priority emerging-market hubs (**Buenos Aires Hub**, **Lagos Hub**, **Nairobi Hub**), one comparative European benchmark (**Barcelona**), and one coordinating team. This forms one trans-local cohort delivering these milestones in unison. No one gets left behind.
3. We aim for organisational excellence. We win and lose together.
