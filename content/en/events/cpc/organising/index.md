# Organising the CPC Fieldwork

This section explains how PRISMA organises the people, timeline and tools behind the CPC research. If you are a Hub Lead, a member of a Local Project Team, or a partner wondering how the work runs, start here.

Phase 3 of the engagement is primary fieldwork. Over six weeks, three [Regional Hubs](/en/collaborators/communities-of-place/hub/hubs) in Lagos, Nairobi and Buenos Aires each coordinate three Local Project Teams. These nine teams interview regulators, payment operators, cooperatives, universities and businesses in their regions. Their findings feed the regional playbooks PRISMA delivers to the Cardano Product Committee, and each hub's own [Case Study](/en/patterns/case-study).

## Principles

Three principles shape how we deliver.

1. **Each hub is an autonomous node in its region.** A hub stewards its local relationships and owns its regional publication.
2. **One cohort, moving together.** Three Regional Hubs, nine Local Project Teams and one coordinating team (Tabs, Alfred, Paul, Doko, Abdulbasit, Ale and Delfina) form a single trans-local cohort. We deliver the milestones in unison, and no one gets left behind.
3. **We win and lose together.** We aim for organisational excellence, and we share the outcome.

## How the pieces fit

The fieldwork follows PRISMA's four core processes:

- **[Enrolment](/en/processes/enrolment)** builds alignment before any interview happens. Hubs and teams register, agree expectations and set their consent boundaries upfront.
- **[Ground Potentialising](/en/processes/ground-potentialising)** makes each hub's readiness, enrolled teams and research focus visible on the shared network view.
- **[Enactment](/en/processes/enactment)** is the fieldwork itself: teams conduct and debrief stakeholder interviews.
- **[Evaluation](/en/processes/evaluation)** cross-checks the evidence and recognises verified contributions.

Underneath, [Process Infrastructuring](/en/processes/process-infrastructuring) gives each hub its own publishing environment. Approved records are signed and stored by the hub on [ATProto](/en/processes/process-infrastructuring/publishing/atproto), they follow the shared templates in the [PRISMA Lexicons](https://github.com/prisma-collective/lexicons), and [Timelining](/en/processes/process-infrastructuring/publishing/timelining) keeps track of team activity so contributions can be valued fairly.
