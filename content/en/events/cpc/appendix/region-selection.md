# **8\. Regional Scope and Prioritisation Plan**

## **8.1 Scope Framing**

The research does not begin with a predetermined geography. The final prioritisation of regions, sub-regions, or country clusters will be determined by RARF scores and primary evidence, not by assumption.

The research will begin from PRISMA's existing network access — primarily sub-Saharan Africa and Latin America — and will systematically evaluate those access points against the RARF criteria before determining whether they represent the highest-value research investment for Cardano. Where the evidence supports a different geographic focus, the screening process will surface it.

## **8.2 Candidate Region Categories**

The minimum regional consideration specified in the RFP comprises four categories. PRISMA's screening will cover all four, with initial depth proportional to existing evidence availability and PRISMA's network access:

| Region Category | Research Purpose | PRISMA Access Level |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| Sub-Saharan Africa / Practical Infrastructure Markets | Agricultural supply chain, identity, public-service delivery, humanitarian payment rails, mobile-first financial inclusion, land registry — contexts where practical infrastructure gaps create genuine demand | High: CATS26 network, Accra ALJ, Nairobi hub relationships, Wada partnership |
| LATAM / High-Inflation Markets | Stablecoin payments, remittances, merchant adoption, community financial infrastructure — contexts where monetary instability creates demand for stable-value settlement | Medium-High: Argentina and broader LATAM ecosystem relationships |
| East Asia / Regulatory-Pilot Markets | Government blockchain pilots, enterprise standards, regulatory sandbox pathways, payment infrastructure — contexts where regulatory appetite for innovation creates structured entry opportunities | Medium: Desk-research primary; primary access to be confirmed and supplemented during Phase 1 |
| Applicant-Justified Additional Region | To be confirmed based on Phase 2 screening — may include Middle East / North Africa, South/Southeast Asia, or specific country clusters that score highly on the RARF across multiple dimensions | To be determined during Phase 1 candidate list construction |

## **8.3 Region Selection Logic**

The following criteria will determine whether a candidate region advances from light screening to deep-dive primary research:

* RARF screening score of 3.0 or above on at least six of ten dimensions, based on desk-evidence

* At least one identifiable, reachable counterparty pathway relevant to the leading use case hypothesis

* A specific use case that is testable within a 12–24 month horizon

* Evidence of real problem severity from sources independent of Cardano community reporting

* No blocking regulatory or infrastructure constraint that cannot be resolved within the research horizon

Regions that fail the screening criteria will be documented in the Market Prioritisation Matrix under Monitor or No-Go recommendations, with explicit reasons. They will not be included in deep-dive primary research. The research budget is allocated proportionally: approximately 60% of primary research time will go to the two highest-scoring regional clusters, with 40% distributed across remaining candidate regions.

## **8.4 Country-Cluster Logic**

Within candidate region categories, the research will work at country-cluster or country level rather than continental level. The RFP explicitly cautions against treating Africa, LATAM, or East Asia as single markets.

Illustrative (not predetermined) country clusters for screening include:

* West Africa: Ghana, Nigeria, Senegal — assessing divergent regulatory environments and ecosystem maturity against a shared regional use-case backdrop

* East Africa: Kenya, Rwanda, Uganda — where mobile money infrastructure and regional regulatory harmonisation may create different prerequisites than West Africa

* Southern Cone / LATAM: Argentina, Uruguay, Paraguay — where high-inflation monetary conditions and dollarisation dynamics create a distinct stablecoin and payment use-case context

* Andean / Pacific LATAM: Colombia, Peru — where agricultural supply chain and financial inclusion use cases may have different evidence profiles than Southern Cone markets

These clusters are illustrative. The actual cluster selection will be confirmed during Phase 1 based on the RARF screening exercise and CPC review.
