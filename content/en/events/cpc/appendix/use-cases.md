# **6.6 Use-Case Assessment Framework**

Use-case assessment for each priority region will evaluate:

* **Problem Severity:** Is the problem the use case addresses genuinely acute in this region? Is there evidence of unmet demand from local actors?

* **Cardano Fit:** Does the specific use case require properties that Cardano provides — deterministic settlement, transparent public ledger, identity primitives, privacy features — or would a centralised or conventional solution serve equally well?

* **Competitive Alternatives:** What alternatives currently exist — both blockchain and non-blockchain — and why or why not would Cardano be preferred?

* **Stakeholder Demand:** Have local actors — not Cardano advocates — expressed specific demand for the kind of solution this use case represents?

* **Dependencies:** Does the use case require stablecoin liquidity, off-ramp access, privacy, identity infrastructure, delivery partners, or regulatory approval before it is actionable?

* **Adoption Pathway:** Is there a concrete pathway from initial pilot to recurring usage, and who owns each step of that pathway?

# **9\. Use-Case Assessment Plan**

## **9.1 Use-Case Selection Logic**

Use cases will be assessed by region rather than globally. The research will not assume that the leading use case in one region is appropriate in another. The use-case assessment will identify which candidate use cases have genuine local problem severity, a credible Cardano fit, reachable counterparties, and a measurable adoption pathway — and which are aspirational without current evidence.

Candidate use cases for screening are drawn directly from the RFP and supplemented by PRISMA's existing ecosystem knowledge:

| Use Case | Key Assessment Question | Primary Evidence Needed |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| Stablecoin payments & settlement | Where do stable-value instruments, local liquidity, and regulatory acceptance combine to make this use case actionable today rather than aspirational? | Stablecoin availability; off-ramp infrastructure; payment regulation; merchant/user demand signal |
| Remittances | Which corridors have the combination of compliance pathway, off-ramp infrastructure, and user-trust conditions to make Cardano-enabled remittance viable? | Corridor volume data; compliance requirements; mobile money infrastructure; user trust evidence |
| Humanitarian payment rails | Which NGO, multilateral, or government actors have the combination of mandate, compliance pathway, and implementation capacity to run a genuine pilot? | NGO/multilateral interviews; compliance requirements; beneficiary access evidence |
| Agricultural supply chain | Where do farmer identity, market access, traceability demand, and local implementation capacity converge into an actionable pilot opportunity? | Agricultural sector interviews; cooperative/aggregator relationships; identity infrastructure assessment |
| Land registry / public records | Where is there genuine government readiness, a legal enforceability pathway, and procurement access — not just theoretical interest? | Government stakeholder interviews; legal enforceability review; procurement pathway assessment |
| Identity / credentials | Where does user need, public-sector acceptance, and wallet/UX infrastructure combine to make a credential use case viable? | Identity regulation review; public-sector acceptance evidence; wallet/UX infrastructure assessment |
| Public procurement / transparency | Where do political feasibility, governance context, and institutional willingness intersect — and where do political-safety risks outweigh the opportunity? | Governance context assessment; political feasibility review; civil society stakeholder interviews |

## **9.2 Use-Case Screening Protocol**

Each use case will be screened against five criteria before being selected for deep-dive analysis. Use cases that fail two or more screening criteria will be documented as deferred or rejected in the Use-Case Viability Assessment deliverable.

* **Genuine Problem Severity:** Evidence from non-Cardano sources that the problem is acute and that current solutions are inadequate

* **Cardano-Specific Advantage:** The use case benefits specifically from Cardano's properties, not simply from any digital ledger

* **Reachable Implementation Pathway:** A concrete route from interest to funded pilot involving identifiable counterparties

* **Infrastructure Prerequisites Met or Resolvable:** The use case does not depend on prerequisites that cannot be satisfied within the research horizon

* **Measurable Adoption Pathway:** A specific definition of what constitutes adoption exists, and it is achievable from a pilot
