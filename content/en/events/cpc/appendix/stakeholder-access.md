# **6.8 Counterparty Intelligence Framework**

The counterparty map for each regional play will distinguish:

* **Decision Sponsors:** The entity with authority to approve or commission a pilot

* **Regulatory / Policy Gatekeepers:** The entity whose approval or non-objection is required before a pilot can proceed

* **Procurement Owners:** The entity through which a public-sector contract would flow

* **Local Delivery Partners:** System integrators, technology providers, or implementation organisations capable of building and operating the solution

* **Distribution and Trust Partners:** Organisations — NGOs, cooperatives, community networks — that provide access, trust, and local legitimacy

* **Technical and Compliance Enablers:** Banks, payment providers, mobile-money operators, legal counsel, identity-service providers

For each named or archetypal counterparty, the map will state whether the entity was interviewed, has been referred by another stakeholder, was desk-identified, or is hypothetical. This distinction is required to ensure that counterparty maps do not overstate access.

# **10\. Stakeholder Access Plan**

## **10.2 Access Limitations and Fallback Protocols**

PRISMA will not overclaim access. Where direct stakeholder access is limited — particularly for senior public-sector officials, central bank regulators, or ministry-level decision-makers — the methodology will:

* Use former officials, policy advisers, and regulatory consultants as proxies, with findings labelled accordingly

* Use structured expert panels to aggregate knowledge where individual interview access is constrained

* Document access limitations explicitly in the Confidence and Limitations Register

* Adjust RARF scores and CASS classifications to reflect access limitations in the evidence base

PRISMA will avoid overstating government interest based on indirect evidence. Where a finding depends on desk-identified or hypothetical counterparties, it will be clearly marked with a lower confidence level and recommended for further diligence before resource deployment.

## **10.3 Managing Insider Bias**

PRISMA's primary research will actively seek non-Cardano stakeholders to balance the evidence base. For each priority region, the research will include at least two respondents with no direct Cardano affiliation — including, where possible, individuals who evaluated Cardano and chose an alternative approach. This is a structural control, not an optional enhancement.
