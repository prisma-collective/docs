# Work Plan and Timeline

## Phase Work Plan

| Phase | Key Activities | Outputs / Checkpoints | Weeks |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| Phase 1: Inception & Design | Kickoff; decision gate alignment; candidate region list; interview guides; screening criteria; bias control protocols; stakeholder recruitment plan; IRB/ethics confirmation | Kickoff Report; Research Design Document; Candidate Region List; Approved Interview Guide | Weeks 1–3 |
| Phase 2: Screening & Desk Research | RARF application to all candidate clusters; regulatory landscape preliminary scan; use-case viability hypothesis construction; counterparty desk mapping; preliminary infrastructure review | Preliminary Market Prioritisation Matrix; Screening Review with CPC; Priority Deep-Dive Selection | Weeks 4–6 |
| Phase 3: Primary Research & Field Access | Stakeholder interviews across all respondent categories; ALJ facilitation in priority hub locations; expert panels; regulatory expert calls; counterparty mapping refinement; infrastructure assessment | Stakeholder Access Check (Week 8); Regulatory & Infrastructure Review (Week 10); Interim Findings Review (Week 12) | Weeks 7–12 |
| Phase 4.1: Analysis & Framework Development | RARF score revision with primary evidence; CASS classification of all signals; playbook drafting; regulatory summary writing; counterparty access register compilation; use-case viability assessment completion | Internal Analysis Memo; Draft Framework Outputs | Weeks 13–15 |
| Phase 4.2: Drafting & Review | Full draft of all core deliverables; CPC draft review; validation against decision gates; revision based on review feedback; public summary preparation | Draft Deliverables Review with CPC (Week 16); Revised Draft | Weeks 15–17 |
| Phase 5: Final Delivery | Final report; executive decision memo; cross-RFP handoff memo; final presentation to CPC; public summary; evidence register; confidence and limitations register | Final Report & Presentation (Week 17–18); Public Summary (Week 18) | Week 18 |

## Milestone Schedule

| Milestone | Timing | Purpose |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| Kickoff | Week 1 | Confirm objectives, decision gates, scope, communication cadence, and confidentiality expectations |
| Research Design Review | End Week 3 | Align on RARF criteria, candidate regions, use-case hypotheses, interview guides, and bias controls before fieldwork begins |
| Regional Screening Review | End Week 6 | Present preliminary RARF scores; confirm priority deep-dive selections; adjust workplan if screening surfaces unexpected constraints |
| Stakeholder Access Check | End Week 8 | Surface any recruitment gaps, access constraints, or regional limitations requiring methodology adaptation |
| Regulatory & Infrastructure Review | End Week 10 | Present initial regulatory findings and infrastructure prerequisite assessments; identify where further expert access is needed |
| Interim Findings Review | End Week 12 | Test whether evidence is answering the decision gates; adjust playbook structure if regional evidence requires it |
| Draft Deliverables Review | End Week 16 | Full draft review: market prioritisation matrix, entry playbooks, regulatory summaries, CASS framework, counterparty map, roadmap |
| Final Report & Presentation | Week 17–18 | Present final findings, confidence levels, limitations, decisions enabled, and recommended actions |
| Public Summary | Week 18 | Confirm what can be published; deliver non-confidential public summary for Cardano community |
