# Milestone 2 - Regional Screening and Desk Research

Milestone 2 - 9,000 ADA

Milestone 2 delivers the regional screening and desk research suite for the study. Using enacted legislation, central bank circulars, multilateral payment data, on-chain stablecoin liquidity figures and local platform benchmarks as of 13 November 2026, this milestone identifies where Cardano can solve real cross-border payment, credential and supply-chain problems today, and where local payment systems or legal restrictions close the door.

Based on these findings, three emerging-market regions anchored by PRISMA's active [Communities of Place](/en/collaborators/communities-of-place/hub/hubs) (**Buenos Aires Hub**, **Lagos Hub** and **Nairobi Hub**) advance to Phase 3 interviews, four candidate regions remain on desk monitoring, and one is excluded.

## Summary of Findings

1. **Three emerging-market regions advance to Phase 3 fieldwork (3 Regional Hubs, 9 Local Project Teams, 42 to 51 target interviews):**
   - **Southern Cone (Argentina, Uruguay, Paraguay - Readiness Score: 3.40 / 5.00):** Coordinated by the **Buenos Aires Hub** (3 Local Teams, 14 to 17 planned interviews). Fieldwork tests dollar settlement escrow for cooperatives and small businesses, along with portable digital credentials paired with contractor payments under Argentina's Decree 70/2023, National Securities Commission Resolution 1058/2025, and Uruguay's Law 20.345.
   - **West Africa (Ghana, Nigeria, Senegal - Readiness Score: 3.25 / 5.00):** Coordinated by the **Lagos Hub** (3 Local Teams, 15 to 18 planned interviews across Lagos, Abuja, Northern Nigeria and Accra). Fieldwork tests wholesale business-to-business currency corridors, credentials issued by universities and trade guilds, and a short 1-to-2 call diagnostic on state land registries under Nigeria's Investments and Securities Act 2025, Nigeria's Data Protection Act 2023, and Ghana's Virtual Asset Service Providers Act 2025 (Act 1154).
   - **East Africa (Kenya, Rwanda, Uganda - Readiness Score: 3.20 / 5.00):** Coordinated by the **Nairobi Hub** (3 Local Teams, 13 to 16 planned interviews across Nairobi, Kigali and Kampala). Fieldwork tests European Union coffee and tea export traceability and regional cross-border settlement under Kenya's Virtual Asset Service Providers Act 2025 (effective 4 November 2025).
2. **Four regions remain on desk monitoring (0 Phase 3 interviews):**
   - **Iberia (Spain - Readiness Score: 3.20 / 5.00):** Domestic payments in Spain are already well served by SEPA Instant and Bizum, and this contract funds three emerging-market fieldwork hubs. Spain stays on desk monitoring as a European reference case for cooperative credentials and municipal participatory-budgeting logs under Spanish procurement law (Law 9/2017 Article 118 and Royal Decree-Law 14/2019).
   - **Brazil (3.05)**, **the Andean countries (2.80)** and **Mexico (2.75):** Held on desk monitoring because domestic payment systems (Pix in Brazil, Yape and Plin in Peru, and SPEI with Bitso in Mexico) already handle everyday transfers at low cost, alongside high central bank capital requirements in Brazil, pending crypto legislation in Colombia, and bank restrictions in Mexico.
3. **One region is excluded (`No-Go`):**
   - **Horn / Ethiopia (2.05):** Excluded from fieldwork because the National Bank of Ethiopia prohibits cryptocurrency settlement and the federal government has consolidated national digital identity and school records onto the state-run Fayda system (Proclamation 1284/2023).
