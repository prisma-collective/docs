## Executive Summary

Phase 2 (Regional Screening and Desk Research) evaluated all eight candidate regional clusters across Latin America, Sub-Saharan Africa, and Southern Europe using enacted statutory texts, central bank circulars, multilateral payment series, on-chain liquidity telemetry, and documented incumbent platform benchmarks as of `2026-11-13` (`REF-01` to `REF-72`):

1. **Three Priority Emerging-Market Regions Advance to Phase 3 Primary Fieldwork (`Conditional-Go`):**
   - **Southern Cone (`Argentina, Uruguay, Paraguay` — Weighted RARF: `3.40 / 5.00` | `68.0 / 100`):** Anchored by the **Buenos Aires Hub** (`12-16` planned interviews testing `H6` B2B cooperative USD escrow and `H7` `QuarkID`/W3C credential portability under `DNU 70/2023`, `CNV Res. 1058/2025`, and Uruguay `Law 20.345`).
   - **West Africa (`Ghana, Nigeria, Senegal` — Weighted RARF: `3.25 / 5.00` | `65.0 / 100`):** Anchored by the **Lagos Hub** (`14-18` planned interviews across Lagos, Abuja, Northern Nigeria, and Accra testing `H1` wholesale B2B/MTO cross-border corridors, `H2` multi-issuer academic and guild credentials, and `H3` `1-2` call diagnostic on state land-registry constraints under Nigeria `ISA 2025`, `NDPA 2023`, and Ghana `Act 1154`).
   - **East Africa (`Kenya, Rwanda, Uganda` — Weighted RARF: `3.20 / 5.00` | `64.0 / 100`):** Anchored by the **Nairobi Hub** (`12-15` planned interviews across Kenya, Rwanda, and Uganda testing `H5` `EUDR` agricultural export traceability and `H4` cross-border EAC/NGO settlement under Kenya's `VASP Act 2025` effective `4 Nov 2025`).
2. **Comparative European Public-Sector Benchmark Retained (`Conditional-Go [Benchmark]`):**
   - **Iberia (`Spain - Barcelona` — Weighted RARF: `3.20 / 5.00` | `64.0 / 100`):** Advances for `3-4` targeted benchmark interviews testing `H11` (`eIDAS 2.0` cooperative credentials) and `H12` (`Decidim` participatory-budgeting audit logs under `LCSP Ley 9/2017 Art. 118` and `Real Decreto-ley 14/2019`).
3. **Three Clusters Retained on Desk Monitoring & One Cluster Excluded (`No-Go`):**
   - **Monitor Clusters:** **Brazil (`3.05`)**, **Andean (`2.80`)**, and **Mexico (`2.75`)** are held at desk monitoring due to domestic payment rail dominance (`Pix`, `Yape/Plin`, `SPEI`/`Bitso`), regulatory barriers (`BCB Res. 520` capital floor, Colombia VASP statutory gap, `Banxico Circular 4/2019`), and absence of funded field hubs.
   - **Excluded Cluster:** **Horn / Ethiopia (`2.05` — `No-Go`)** is excluded from active entry due to the National Bank of Ethiopia's cryptocurrency settlement prohibition and federal consolidation of national digital ID and education records onto `Fayda` (`MOSIP`, `Proclamation 1284/2023`).
