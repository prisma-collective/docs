# Milestone 5 — Final Delivery

Milestone 5 — ADA 8,000

The final milestone covers the delivery of revised final outputs, the public summary, the final presentation, and the archiving of the evidence register and supporting documentation. Payment is released upon CPC's written confirmation of project completion — a straightforward procedural confirmation that the agreed deliverables have been received and that the public summary has been cleared.

The back-weighting of this milestone reflects standard grant practice: holding a meaningful final payment against confirmed completion creates accountability without being punitive. ADA 8,000 represents approximately 14% of total budget held at the final gate — sufficient to ensure completion without creating cash-flow conditions that would prevent PRISMA from resourcing the work adequately in earlier phases.
