# Milestone 1 - Inception and Research Design

Milestone 1 - 7,000 ADA (Approved)

Milestone 1 set the research design, scoring frameworks and privacy safeguards for the whole study before any fieldwork began. The Kickoff Report aligned PRISMA and the Cardano Product Committee on the five-phase schedule and deliverables. The Research Design Document defined the ten-dimension Regional Adoption Readiness Framework, the four-tier Cardano Adoption Signal Standard, the five counterparty access levels, the eight candidate regions, the fifteen initial use-case hypotheses, and the field bias controls.

Milestone 1 also established the participant consent options and encrypted hub vault rules that protect interviewees before any conversation is recorded.

## Next Step: Milestone 2
