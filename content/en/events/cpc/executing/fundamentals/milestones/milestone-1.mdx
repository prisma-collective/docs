# Milestone 1 — Initial Commitments

Milestone 1 — ADA 7,000 (Approved)

The first milestone covers the design and governance infrastructure of the project. Nothing in the research is irreversible at this stage; the budget is used for team time in research design, not for field commitments. The Kickoff Report confirms that the team, the CPC, and the project structure are aligned before any significant resource is deployed. The Research Design Document — particularly the RARF criteria, the candidate region list, and the bias control protocols — was reviewed and approved by the CPC before Phase 2 began.

The ethics brief, also completed for M1, establishes the consent management, data classification, and respondent protection protocols before any primary research interaction occurs.
