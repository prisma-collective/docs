# Stakeholder Ecosystem and Counterparty Mapping Report

This report translates the Phase 2 regional desk screening into an operational institutional recruitment slate for Phase 3 primary fieldwork (Weeks 7-12), following the sampling framework established in the Milestone 1 [Stakeholder Recruitment Plan](../milestone-1/research-design-document/stakeholder-recruitment-plan.md) and [Bias Control](../milestone-1/research-design-document/bias-control.md) specifications (`REF-72`).

All 64 entries in this register represent pre-interview institutional mappings captured as of `2026-11-13` (`0 Interviewed`). Primary stakeholder interviews begin in Phase 3.

---

## 1. Sampling Methodology and Recruitment Structure

To ensure that Phase 3 primary research captures representative operational, regulatory, and technical perspectives across each advancing market, the counterparty slate is structured around five methodological design principles:

1. **Five-Tier Access Taxonomy (`0 Interviewed` at Milestone 2):** Every mapped organization is classified according to its current recruitment status prior to Phase 3 outreach:
   - `Tier 1: Interviewed` (`0` entries at Milestone 2; populated only after informed consent, completed interview conduct, and cross-hub record verification during Phase 3);
   - `Tier 2: Warm Access` (`18` entries: direct institutional or professional contact held by the research hubs in **Buenos Aires**, **Lagos**, **Nairobi**, or **Barcelona**);
   - `Tier 3: Referred` (`18` entries: named introduction pathway through an established first-degree industry, cooperative, legal, or academic contact);
   - `Tier 4: Desk-Identified` (`23` entries: verified public agency, regulatory directorate, or commercial operator with a documented consultation channel, awaiting formal outreach);
   - `Tier 5: Hypothetical` (`5` entries: central-government tender authority or central-bank gatekeeper included for structural completeness where 12-24 month pilot reachability is low).
2. **Six-Role Institutional Value-Chain Coverage (`R1` to `R6`):** Moving a regional use case from concept to pilot requires alignment across six distinct institutional functions:
   - `R1: Decision Sponsor` (ministry directorate, municipal secretariat, cooperative board, export union executive);
   - `R2: Regulatory Gatekeeper` (central bank, securities commission, data protection authority, specialist legal counsel);
   - `R3: Procurement / Budget Owner` (public procurement agency, municipal innovation fund, humanitarian grant owner);
   - `R4: Local Delivery Partner` (system integrator, software cooperative, agritech or civic-tech engineering team);
   - `R5: Distribution / Trust Partner` (agricultural cooperative federation, SME trade association, public university network, civic federation);
   - `R6: Technical / Compliance Enabler` (licensed `VASP/PSAV` off-ramp, `USSD` and mobile-money aggregator, biometric KYC verification provider).
3. **Inclusion of Non-Cardano Market Operators:** Each advancing regional slate includes at least two high-priority **Non-Cardano Counterweights**, defined as commercial operators or institutional platforms currently running on competing blockchain networks (`Tron TRC-20`, `Polygon`, `Celo MiniPay`, `ZKsync Era`) or established Web2 payment and identity systems (`NIBSS NIP`, `NIMC NIN VSPs`, `M-Pesa`, `Transferencias 3.0`, `Decidim`). Interviewing these operators directly tests whether Cardano offers a measurable cost, compliance, or auditability advantage over existing infrastructure.
4. **Independence Balance and Conflict-of-Interest Disclosure:** Across the 64 mapped organizations, `50` (`78.1%`) are independent non-Cardano institutions (`coi_flag: false`) and `14` (`21.9%`) have prior Cardano, `Wada`, or regional summit (`CATS26`) affiliation (`coi_flag: true`). During Phase 3 fieldwork, Cardano-affiliated participants are capped at no more than `25%` of completed interviews (`target <= 20%`).
5. **Institutional Archetype Framing Prior to Consent:** In accordance with Milestone 1 [Ethics](../milestone-1/research-design-document/ethics.md) and statutory data protection requirements (`NDPA 2023`, `Kenya DPA 2019`, `Argentina Law 25.326`, `EU GDPR`), no individual's personal name or private contact information is published in this repository. Entries identify the institutional archetype, regulatory mandate, and representative organizational examples.

## 4. Phase 3 Recruitment, Substitution, and Quality-Control Rules

During the Phase 3 fieldwork sprint across **Buenos Aires Hub**, **Lagos Hub**, **Nairobi Hub**, and the **Barcelona** EU Benchmark (Weeks 7-12), field teams apply four operational rules established in the Milestone 1 [Stakeholder Recruitment Plan](../milestone-1/research-design-document/stakeholder-recruitment-plan.md):

1. **Regional Interview Quotas (`>= 30` Contractual Floor; `34-54` Target Range):**
   - **Buenos Aires Hub** (`Southern Cone`): `10 - 16` completed interviews across at least four Question Pack categories;
   - **Lagos Hub** (`West Africa` - Lagos, Abuja, Northern Nigeria academic corridor, and Accra corridor): `12 - 18` completed interviews across at least four Question Pack categories;
   - **Nairobi Hub** (`East Africa` - Nairobi, Central Kenya export cooperatives, and Kigali/Kampala EAC corridors): `10 - 15` completed interviews across at least four Question Pack categories;
   - **Barcelona** (`Iberia Comparative EU Benchmark`): `2 - 3` completed benchmark interviews (`H11`, `H12`);
   - **Monitor Clusters** (`Brazil`, `Andean`): `0 - 2` opportunistic interviews if a referred target responds without diverting emerging-market hub bandwidth.
2. **Same-Archetype Substitution Protocol (`7-Day Response Window`):**
   - With 64 mapped organizations supporting a 34-54 interview target (`~1.5x` buffer), if a `Tier 2 (Warm Access)` or `Tier 3 (Referred)` organization does not confirm scheduling within seven calendar days of initial outreach in Weeks 7-8, the regional hub lead substitutes an alternate institution of the same functional role (`R1-R6`), Question Pack category (`1-7`), and independence classification (`coi_flag: false`) within the same cluster.
3. **Completion of Non-Cardano Counterweight Interviews:**
   - Each priority hub must complete interviews with at least two designated Non-Cardano Counterweights (`CP-SC-06` and `CP-SC-07` in Southern Cone; `CP-WA-03` and `CP-WA-07` in West Africa; `CP-EA-02` and `CP-EA-03` in East Africa; `CP-IB-01` and `CP-IB-02` in Iberia) prior to closing its Phase 3 sample.
4. **Role Segregation and Cross-Hub Verification:**
   - Where a participant was introduced through a regional hub or ecosystem network (`PRISMA-HUB`, `CATS26-WARM`, `WADA-NET`, or `CARDANO-ECO`), the team member who made the introduction cannot act as sole interviewer or verify the resulting interview record. Every Phase 3 interview record undergoes cross-hub verification prior to publication ([Data Ingestion Schema and Fieldwork Verification Protocol](/en/events/cpc/executing/deliverables/phase-2-schema-and-protocol-update)).
