# Research Design Document

## Purpose

This is the CPC approval surface for Phase 1 design. It frames the linked artefacts below; it does not replace them. CPC approval of this package unlocks Phase 2 screening. Verification criteria for M1 are summarised in [Milestone 1](/en/events/cpc/executing/fundamentals/milestones/milestone-1).

## Design stance

The research is mixed-method and hypothesis-testing: desk screening first, primary evidence to test and revise, then decision-ready synthesis. It is organised around the RFP decision gates, not a predetermined geography or conclusion. See [Thesis](/en/events/cpc/executing/thesis) and [Phases](/en/events/cpc/executing/phases).

## Decision gates

Every workstream exists to answer the eight gates in [Decision Gates](/en/events/cpc/executing/terminology/decision-gates). No section of the research exists except to answer one or more of them.

## Screening instrument (RARF)

The [Regional Adoption Readiness Framework](/en/events/cpc/executing/terminology/adoption-readiness) is the comparative scoring model for candidate clusters. This design applies it as desk scores with explicit confidence first, then revises scores after primary research with changes tracked. The framework is intended to remain reusable after this engagement.

## Adoption signal control (CASS)

The [Cardano Adoption Signal Standard](/en/events/cpc/executing/terminology/adoption-signals) is the control against treating intent, events, or informal interest as adoption. Every identified signal is classified to evidence-supported tier only.
