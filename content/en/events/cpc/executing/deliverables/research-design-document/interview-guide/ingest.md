# Layer 2: Vault Review, Redaction and Repository Publishing

Within **48 hours** of an interview in the **Lagos Hub**, **Nairobi Hub** or **Buenos Aires Hub**, the Local Project Team interviewer and the hub's designated Vault Reviewer turn the raw recording or notes into a structured, privacy-checked research record using PRISMA's [Process Infrastructuring](/en/processes/process-infrastructuring) and [AT Protocol Publishing](/en/processes/process-infrastructuring/publishing/atproto) tools.

---

### 1. How the Hub Vault Web Form and 48-hour review work

1. **Wallet sign-in and upload to the encrypted Hub Vault (`Private` by default).**
   - The Local Project Team signs in to the **Hub Vault Web Form** with its dedicated project wallet and uploads the interview recording and structured notes (`events-cli` integration is documented separately as confirmed by the CLI engineering lead).
   - Every new file starts as **Private** ("zero raw data leaves the hub vault"). Original audio, video and unedited transcripts never leave the vault under any [Consent Choice](/en/events/cpc/organising/enrolment/form-guidance/local-stakeholders).
   - **Launch gate and recorded video walkthrough (19 to 21 November):** During the 19 to 21 November dry run, the Infrastructure & Regional Hub Leads test the full pipeline end to end using the [Synthetic Interview Test Samples](/en/events/cpc/organising/synthetic-interview-test-samples) and record a short end-to-end screen-recording demonstration video (wallet sign-in, synthetic interview upload, local filter check, and `/review` sign-off) to onboard all nine Local Project Teams. Real interviews recorded from 21 November onwards are uploaded to the Hub Vault Web Form and remain held strictly as **Private** inside the vault until (a) the four-choice consent specification (`Private`, `Research`, `Anonymous public`, `Public named`) is formally locked and (b) the local transcription and redaction filter step is live and verified on the pilot server.
2. **On-server transcription and identifier flagging.**
   - Once the filter step is active on the hub server, Whisper runs locally to transcribe the audio in memory without writing plain audio to disk.
   - Fixed regex rules and a local language model on the hub server highlight personal names, job titles, contact details (including spaced phone digits, URLs, or spelled-out email addresses) and any third parties mentioned during the conversation.
3. **Joint human review and redaction on `/review`.**
   - The interviewer and the Hub Vault Reviewer inspect the draft together on the vault review screen (`/review`) and fill in the structured findings.
   - **Hard redaction check:** if the participant chose an anonymous option (`Research` [maps to M1 Confidential Synthesis] or `Anonymous public` [maps to M1 Role-Only Anonymised]), the vault review screen blocks approval while any flagged name, third-party reference or contact detail remains unredacted. Reviewers can keep the participant's pre-recorded visibility choice or make it more private, never more public.
4. **Where the finished record lives (and post-ingest `CIP-0020` batch anchoring).**
   - **`Private` and `Research` records:** stay permanently inside the **Encrypted Hub Vault** (`apps/vault`) and are never written to an AT Protocol repository. `Research` transcripts are available only to the Cardano Product Committee and authorised project researchers through the vault's authenticated login.
   - **`Anonymous public` and `Public named` records (once consent and filter gates are locked):** the Hub Vault Reviewer signs in with their `at-pds` wallet session (`authorDid`) and publishes the approved record (keyed by the recording's `media.sha256` fingerprint, with `consentScope` set to `"anonymous"` for `Anonymous public` or `"public"` for `Public named` [maps to M1 Full Attribution]) into the Local Team's repository on the hub server (`at-pds`). The Relay, Tap and the AppView verify the signature and index the record for [Timelining](/en/processes/process-infrastructuring/publishing/timelining) and [Case Study](/en/patterns/case-study) synthesis.
   - **Post-ingest `CIP-0020` batch anchoring:** Cardano `CIP-0020` on-chain transaction metadata batching is **not** a prerequisite blocker for starting real interviews on 21 November; it follows asynchronously after the first verified ingest and publication wave.

### 2. Lexicon structure (`events.prisma.evidence.interview`), `eventId` namespace finalisation, and analytical fields

In `at-pds` and `at-tap` today, the working interview record structure is `events.prisma.evidence.interview` (`text`, `consentScope` [currently `"anonymous"` or `"public"`, expanding to the full four-choice consent model once locked], `topic: "interview"`, `media: { sha256, mimeType, kind }`, `recordedAt`, `authorDid`, `createdAt`), alongside `events.prisma.hub.profile` and `events.prisma.hub.team` ([PRISMA Lexicons](https://github.com/prisma-collective/at-tap/tree/main/lexicons/events/prisma/evidence)).

**Pending `eventId` namespace finalisation and Lexicon PR:** The final Lexicon namespace for interview records is currently being finalised by the Infrastructure Lead (Tobias Fechner) to incorporate `eventId` directly into the namespace. Extended research analysis fields (such as Decision Gate tags, regulatory certainty pairings, and provisional CASS ratings) will be introduced via a dedicated Lexicon pull request once the core `eventId` namespace is locked. Until that Lexicon pull request lands, the five analytical sections below are captured inside the encrypted vault draft and structured into the approved `text` markdown body:

* **1. Provenance and consent header:** the participant's withdrawal reference code, the hub (`events.prisma.hub.profile`) and team (`events.prisma.hub.team`) repository identifiers, the reviewer's dedicated project wallet identifier (`authorDid`, [Cardano DIDs](/en/processes/enrolment/dids/overview)), the region and city, the Question Pack used, the interviewee's functional role, whether the organisation is independent of Cardano, the chosen consent level (`Private`, `Research`, `Anonymous public` [`consentScope: "anonymous"`], or `Public named` [`consentScope: "public"`]), the AI training setting (`false` by default), and the cryptographic vault fingerprint (`media.sha256` and 12-month deletion ceiling).
* **2. Hypothesis test summary:** which regional use case was tested, whether the conversation confirmed, revised, rejected or left the desk hypothesis inconclusive, which local incumbent system was compared, whether the falsification condition was triggered, and a plain-English summary of the outcome.
* **3. Evidence across the eight decision gates:** structured findings linked to the relevant decision gates and Regional Adoption Readiness dimensions, with redacted quotes and a confidence rating (`High`, `Medium` or `Low`).
* **4. Adoption signal rating:** a description of any concrete adoption signal observed, the type of evidence behind it, the proposed Cardano Adoption Signal Standard tier (`Tier 1` to `Tier 4`), and whether independent corroboration is still needed.
* **5. Legal, infrastructure and cross-RFP notes:** any statute or central bank circular cited, whether a local lawyer needs to confirm the point, ratings across the eight infrastructure categories (`Met`, `Partially Met`, `Unmet`, `Unknown` or `Dependent on Another Workstream`), warm introductions offered, and handoffs to other Cardano Product Committee research tracks.

For the full JSON schema and protocol details, see the [Data Ingestion Schema and Fieldwork Verification Protocol](/en/events/cpc/executing/deliverables/phase-2-schema-and-protocol-update). For the three ready-to-use synthetic interview test packages for the 19 to 21 November vault dry run, see [Synthetic Interview Test Samples](/en/events/cpc/organising/synthetic-interview-test-samples).
