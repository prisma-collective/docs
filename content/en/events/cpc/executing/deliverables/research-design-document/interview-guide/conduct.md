# Layer 1: Interview Conduct Protocol

### Before the Interview

* **Assemble** the interview brief: one **play** (`cluster x use case`) x one [Question Pack](/en/events/cpc/organising/question-packs) (respondent type). Fill the pack's `Assemble` fields before the call using the [Use-Case Hypothesis Register](/en/events/cpc/executing/deliverables/use-case-hypothesis-register).
* Assign the participant's deterministic **`Respondent_ID` / Withdrawal Reference Number** (e.g., `SC-BA-PAY-01`, `WA-LOS-REG-02`, `EA-NRB-AGR-01`).
* Run the respondent screener (`Regional/Play Fit`, `Functional Category`, `Operational Authority`, `Non-Cardano Independence Check`).
* Read the 60-second [Consent Read-Aloud Script (`D30`)](/en/events/cpc/organising/enrolment/form-guidance/local-stakeholders) and **record the participant's consent choice before starting any recording**:
  * **`1. Private` *(Safe Default)*:** Stays inside the encrypted Hub Vault for the interviewing team only; never quoted or published.
  * **`2. Research` *(CPC / Authorised Researchers)*:** Edited anonymous transcript shared only with the CPC and authorised researchers from inside the Hub Vault; never published on the public internet or any AT Protocol repo.
  * **`3. Anonymous public`:** Approved anonymous quotes and structured gate findings published to the Team's AT Protocol repo (`events.prisma.evidence.interview`).
  * **`4. Public, named`:** Approved statements published alongside the participant's name, title, and organisation in the Team's AT Protocol repo.
  * **Separate AI Model Training Toggle (`Default: No`):** Record whether the participant permits future AI model training (`No` by default).

### Opening (All Categories)

1. Thank the participant and confirm voluntary participation.
2. State the research purpose: regional adoption readiness and operational barrier assessment for Cardano government and emerging-market entry.
3. State the commissioning context: Cardano Product Committee research (`RFP 05`).
4. Provide the participant's **Withdrawal Reference Number** (`Respondent_ID`) and confirm they may withdraw at any time across all four consent choices (with all raw files deleted within 12 months of project completion).
5. Confirm the selected `D30` consent choice (`1. Private` default, `2. Research`, `3. Anonymous public`, or `4. Public, named`) and AI toggle (`No` default).
6. Obtain explicit permission to proceed and start recording.

### Core Evidence Capture (All Categories)

During the conversation, keep probes anchored to the **assembled play**. Map evidence to:

| Capture Target | Interviewer Action |
| :---- | :---- |
| Decision gates addressed | Tag each substantive finding to one or more gates (`G1-G8`) *for this play* |
| Counterparty intelligence | Name or archetype; functional role (`1 of 6 roles`) per [Stakeholder Access](/en/events/cpc/appendix/stakeholder-access); access basis |
| Adoption signals | Describe signal; propose CASS tier (`Tier 1-4`) with evidence justification (only if a concrete signal appears) |
| Regulatory findings | Cite specific statute/circular; apply three-tier certainty (`Clear / Evolving / Uncertain`) and local counsel flag |
| Infrastructure prerequisites | Note stablecoin liquidity, fiat off-ramp, wallet UX, mobile/offline, identity, privacy, delivery, or SLA constraints |
| Play disposition | `Confirmed` / `Revised` / `Rejected` / `Inconclusive` after the call |
| Referrals | Capture warm introductions with explicit permission to name the referrer |

Category-specific probes: [Question Packs](/en/events/cpc/organising/question-packs). Prioritise each pack's primary gates; complete `Capture` fields during the 48-hour vault review.

### Closing (All Categories)

1. Ask whether any operational, regulatory, or commercial barrier was missed.
2. Confirm willingness to be re-contacted for fact-checking or quote verification.
3. Ask for referrals to other relevant stakeholders (with permission to name the referrer).
4. Re-confirm the participant's **Withdrawal Reference Number** (`Respondent_ID`) and post-interview vault handling.
5. Thank the participant.
