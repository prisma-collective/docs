# Layer 1: Preparing and Conducting the Interview

### Before the call

* **Prepare the interview brief** during [Ground Potentialising](/en/processes/ground-potentialising): choose the regional workflow being tested from the [Use-Case Hypothesis Register](/en/events/cpc/executing/deliverables/use-case-hypothesis-register) and pair it with the matching [Question Pack](/en/events/cpc/organising/question-packs) for the interviewee's role. Fill in the preparation block at the top of the pack before the meeting.
* **Create the participant's withdrawal reference number** so they have a simple code they can quote if they ever want to update or remove their interview.
* **Confirm respondent fit:** check that the interviewee has direct operational knowledge of the workflow, works in the region under study, and is recorded accurately under our 75% non-Cardano independence rule.
* **Read the consent script and record the participant's choice before switching on the recorder** ([Local Stakeholder Consent Guide](/en/events/cpc/organising/enrolment/form-guidance/local-stakeholders)):
  * **1. Private (the default):** stays inside the encrypted Hub Vault for internal team calibration only; never quoted or published.
  * **2. Research (Cardano Product Committee and authorised researchers):** names and contact details are removed; the anonymous transcript can be read only by the Cardano Product Committee and authorised project researchers inside the Hub Vault, never on the public web or an AT Protocol repository.
  * **3. Anonymous public:** names and contact details are removed; approved anonymous quotes and structured findings are published to the team's repository.
  * **4. Public, named:** statements approved by the participant are published with their name, role and organisation in the team's repository.
  * **Separate AI model training question (No by default):** record whether the participant allows future external AI model training.

---

### Opening the interview (all respondent categories)

1. Thank the participant and confirm that taking part is voluntary.
2. State the purpose of the research: testing practical payment, credential and compliance workflows across West Africa (**Lagos Hub**), East Africa (**Nairobi Hub**) and the Southern Cone (**Buenos Aires Hub**).
3. Explain the commissioning context: research carried out for the Cardano Product Committee under RFP 05 (Grant CPC-26-0013).
4. Give the participant their **withdrawal reference number** and confirm that they can change their consent level or withdraw at any time, and that raw recordings are deleted within 12 months of project completion.
5. Confirm their chosen visibility level (Private, Research, Anonymous public, or Public, named) and their answer to the AI training question.
6. Ask permission to begin and start the recording ([Enactment](/en/processes/enactment)).

---

### What to capture during the conversation

Keep questions grounded in how the interviewee's organisation actually operates today and compare any blockchain workflow directly with the local systems they already use:

| Area to cover | What the interviewer records |
| :---- | :---- |
| **Decision gates** | Link each practical finding to the relevant research gates (regional fit, use-case economics, counterparty authority, legal rules, infrastructure gaps, delivery model, adoption signals, or links to other RFPs). |
| **Counterparty role** | Note the organisation's operational role (one of the six roles defined in [Stakeholder Access](/en/events/cpc/appendix/stakeholder-access)) and how the contact was reached. |
| **Adoption signals** | If a real project or pilot exists, describe it and note the evidence behind its Cardano Adoption Signal Standard tier (transaction hashes, a signed pilot contract, or a verbal statement). |
| **Regulatory rules** | Note the specific law, central bank circular or licensing requirement mentioned, how clear it is in practice, and whether a local lawyer needs to confirm it. |
| **Infrastructure gaps** | Record practical bottlenecks in stablecoin liquidity, local currency cash-out, wallet usability, mobile or offline access, digital identity, privacy, local engineering support or uptime. |
| **Outcome for the hypothesis** | Note after the call whether the evidence confirmed, revised, rejected or left the desk hypothesis inconclusive. |
| **Warm introductions** | Ask who else we should speak with and whether the interviewee is comfortable being named when we reach out ([Ecosystem Weaving](/en/collaborators/core/business-development/alfred/ecosystem-weaving)). |

For role-specific questions, see the seven [Question Packs](/en/events/cpc/organising/question-packs). Everything captured here is reviewed and formatted within 48 hours in [Layer 2: Ingest](/en/events/cpc/executing/deliverables/research-design-document/interview-guide/ingest).

---

### Closing the interview

1. Ask whether there is any regulatory, technical or commercial obstacle we have not asked about.
2. Check whether the participant is happy to be contacted briefly if we need to verify a fact or check a quote.
3. Ask for introductions to other relevant people in the sector, and whether we may mention their name when reaching out.
4. Remind the participant of their **withdrawal reference number** and how their record will be handled in the Hub Vault.
5. Thank the participant and close the call.
