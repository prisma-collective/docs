# Statutory, Institutional and Technical Reference Registry

This reference registry indexes all 72 secondary and tertiary sources cited across the Phase 2 (Milestone 2) regional screening deliverables for Grant `CPC-26-0013` (RFP 05 core; RFP 02 and RFP 01 supporting). All protocol telemetry, stablecoin supply figures, regulatory enactment statuses, and local platform metrics carry an explicit snapshot capture date of `2026-11-13`. Zero primary stakeholder interviews were conducted during Phase 2 (`0 Interviewed`).

## 1. Source Classification and Evidence Governance

In accordance with the Milestone 1 [Data Governance Protocol](/en/events/cpc/appendix/data), every source in this registry is classified across three parameters:

1. **Source Category:**
   - `Public Secondary - Statutory / Regulatory`: Enacted parliamentary statutes, presidential decrees, central bank circulars, securities commission rulebooks, and data protection authority guidance.
   - `Public Secondary - Multilateral / Official Statistics`: World Bank, IMF, BIS, GSMA, national identity agencies, and national payment switch statistical bulletins.
   - `Public Secondary - Industry / Competitor Telemetry`: Independent market analytics (Chainalysis, DeFiLlama) and documented technical specifications of existing Web2 platforms or competing blockchain networks.
   - `Public Secondary - Ecosystem / Applicant Technical`: Published Cardano Improvement Proposals (CIPs), open-source middleware repositories (`Hyperledger Identus`, `Veridian`, `Midnight`, `Orcfax`), and open-source research verification infrastructure (`at-tap`, `at-pds`).
2. **Independence Classification:**
   - `Independent`: Produced by a legislature, regulator, multilateral agency, national payment switch, or non-Cardano commercial operator with zero financial interest in Cardano adoption.
   - `Ecosystem Primary`: Produced by a Cardano ecosystem entity or protocol team. Used strictly to verify technical capabilities or evaluate historical ecosystem claims, never as standalone proof of regional market demand.
   - `Applicant Technical`: Produced by Aspecting OÜ (trading as PRISMA) or its open-source engineering contributors (`prisma-collective/at-tap` and `prisma-collective/at-pds`).
3. **Desk Confidence Calibration:**
   - Statutory texts and central bank circulars carry `Medium (Secondary Data Only)` confidence regarding enacted legal rules, paired with `Low (Pre-Interview Desk Estimate)` confidence regarding practical administrative enforcement and commercial bank compliance posture until tested in Phase 3 interviews.
