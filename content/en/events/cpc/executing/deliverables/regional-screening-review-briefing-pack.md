## 1. Purpose of the Phase 2 Regional Screening Review

At the conclusion of Phase 2 desk research (Week 6), the **Regional Screening Review** consolidates the comparative evaluation of all eight candidate regional clusters and confirms which three priority emerging-market hubs advance to Phase 3 primary fieldwork (`30-45` minimum interviews, `42-51` target interactions across `9 Local Project Teams` in Weeks 7-12).

The review process comprises two complementary stages:

1. **Cross-Hub Research Synthesis (`Completed 2026-11-12`):** Conducted jointly by the Principal Investigator (`Tobias Fechner`), the Co-Principal Investigator and Project Lead (`Alfred Itodole`), and the regional hub leads in **Buenos Aires**, **Lagos**, and **Nairobi**. The team audited all 80 provisional RARF cells ([Regional Market Prioritisation Matrix](/en/events/cpc/executing/deliverables/market-prioritisation-matrix)), the four alternative weighting sensitivity checks, the ten statutory verification questions in the [Preliminary Regulatory Scan](/en/events/cpc/executing/deliverables/regulatory-preliminary-scan), the fifteen use-case hypotheses in the [Use-Case Viability Register](/en/events/cpc/executing/deliverables/desk-research-and-use-case-scan), and the 64-entry recruitment slate ([Counterparty Mapping Report](/en/events/cpc/executing/deliverables/counterparty-access-map)).
2. **Portfolio Checkpoint Briefing:** This briefing summarizes the five Phase 2 portfolio decisions, the eight-cluster comparative scorecard, and the Phase 3 hypothesis falsification slate. Any feedback on regional interview quotas, hypothesis scope, or `Monitor` cluster priorities received during Milestone 2 review will be incorporated into the Week 7 pre-interview `Assemble` templates.

## 5. Phase 3 Implementation Timeline (`Weeks 7-12`)

1. **Week 7 (`Stage 1 - Hub Onboarding & Outreach`):** Initiate the institutional outreach schedule across the **Buenos Aires Hub**, **Lagos Hub**, and **Nairobi Hub** (`3 Local Project Teams per hub = 9 Local Teams` total); provision hub and field-team Decentralized Identifiers (`did:plc` paired with dedicated `did:cardano:stake1...` wallets).
2. **Week 8 (`Stage 2 - Researcher Calibration & Assemble Lock`):** Complete the five researcher calibration modules; finalize pre-call Question Pack `Assemble` blocks for scheduled participants across the seven active workflows; trigger 7-day same-archetype substitutions for any non-responsive `Warm Access` or `Referred` organizations.
3. **Weeks 9-10 (`Stage 3 - Fieldwork Conduct, Ingestion & Review`):** Conduct `42-51` target stakeholder interactions (`>= 30` contractual floor) across **Buenos Aires Hub (`14-17`)**, **Lagos Hub (`15-18`)**, and **Nairobi Hub (`13-16`)**, storing raw recordings in encrypted local hub vaults within 48 hours and completing independent reviewer verification and co-signing (`ingest.json` v2.0 `strongRef` + `recordingHash`) within 72 hours.
4. **Weeks 11-12 (`Stage 4 - Triangulation & Milestone 3 Synthesis`):** Execute cross-hub triangulation and hypothesis falsification reviews, update RARF and CASS ratings with primary evidence, and deliver the **Milestone 3 Primary Fieldwork Synthesis Report (`22,000.00 ADA`)**.
