## 1. Purpose of the Phase 2 Regional Screening Review

At the conclusion of Phase 2 desk research (Week 6), the **Regional Screening Review** consolidates the comparative evaluation of all eight candidate regional clusters and confirms which three priority emerging-market hubs advance to Phase 3 primary fieldwork (`30-45` minimum interviews, `42-52` target interactions across Weeks 7-12, `REF-72`).

The review process comprises two complementary stages:

1. **Cross-Hub Research Synthesis (`Completed 2026-11-12`):** Conducted jointly by the Principal Investigator (`Alfred Itodole`), the Project Director (`Tobias Fechner`), and the regional hub leads in **Buenos Aires**, **Lagos**, **Nairobi**, and **Barcelona**. The team audited all 80 provisional RARF cells ([Regional Market Prioritisation Matrix](/en/events/cpc/executing/deliverables/market-prioritisation-matrix)), four weighting sensitivity models (`S1-S4`), ten statutory verification triggers (`LC-01` to `LC-10` in the [Preliminary Regulatory Scan](/en/events/cpc/executing/deliverables/regulatory-preliminary-scan)), fifteen use-case hypotheses (`H1-H15` in the [Use-Case Viability Register](/en/events/cpc/executing/deliverables/desk-research-and-use-case-scan)), and the 64-entry recruitment slate ([Counterparty Mapping Report](/en/events/cpc/executing/deliverables/counterparty-access-map)).
2. **Portfolio Checkpoint Briefing:** This briefing summarizes the five Phase 2 portfolio decisions, the eight-cluster comparative scorecard, and the Phase 3 hypothesis falsification slate. Any feedback on regional interview quotas, hypothesis scope, or `Monitor` cluster priorities received during Milestone 2 review will be incorporated into the Week 7 pre-interview `Assemble` templates.

1. **Week 7 (`Stage 1 - Hub Onboarding & Outreach`):** Initiate the 64-organization outreach schedule across **Buenos Aires Hub**, **Lagos Hub**, **Nairobi Hub**, and **Barcelona**; provision hub and field-team Decentralized Identifiers (`did:plc` paired with `did:cardano:stake1...`).
2. **Week 8 (`Stage 2 - Researcher Calibration & Assemble Lock`):** Complete the five researcher calibration modules; finalize pre-call Question Pack `Assemble` blocks for scheduled participants; trigger 7-day same-archetype substitutions for any non-responsive `Tier 2` or `Tier 3` organizations.
3. **Weeks 9-10 (`Stage 3 - Fieldwork Conduct, Ingestion & Review`):** Conduct `42-52` target stakeholder interactions (`>= 30` contractual floor), storing raw recordings in encrypted local hub vaults within 48 hours and completing independent reviewer verification and co-signing (`ingest.json` v2.0 `strongRef` + `recordingHash`) within 72 hours.
4. **Weeks 11-12 (`Stage 4 - Triangulation & Milestone 3 Synthesis`):** Execute cross-hub triangulation and hypothesis falsification reviews, update RARF and CASS ratings with primary evidence, and deliver the **Milestone 3 Primary Fieldwork Synthesis Report (`22,000.00 ADA`)**.
