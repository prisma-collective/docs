# Layer 1 : Interview Conduct Protocol

### Before the Interview

* Confirm participant has received the consent and information sheet
* Confirm recording preference (recorded / notes only / anonymous)
* Confirm whether attribution is permitted in outputs
* Identify respondent category and access basis at point of contact
* Confirm regional cluster and use-case relevance against [Candidate Regions](/en/events/cpc/executing/deliverables/candidate-regions)
* Run the screener (Section 7) if not already completed at scheduling

### Opening (All Categories)

1. Thank the participant and confirm voluntary participation
2. State the research purpose: regional adoption readiness assessment for Cardano government and emerging-market entry
3. State the commissioning context: Cardano Product Committee research (RFP 05)
4. Explain how findings will be used: internal decision support; public summary excludes confidential material
5. Confirm confidentiality treatment and right to withdraw
6. Ask permission to proceed

### Core Evidence Capture (All Categories)

During the conversation, the interviewer actively maps evidence to:

| Capture Target | Interviewer Action |
| :---- | :---- |
| Decision gates addressed | Tag each substantive finding to one or more gates |
| Counterparty intelligence | Name or archetype; role type per [Stakeholder Access](/en/events/cpc/appendix/stakeholder-access); access basis |
| Adoption signals | Describe signal; propose CASS tier with evidence justification |
| Regulatory findings | Apply three-tier certainty (Clear / Evolving / Uncertain) where relevant |
| Infrastructure prerequisites | Note mobile, identity, payment, wallet UX, or delivery constraints |
| Referrals | Capture with referrer permission |

Category-specific question banks are maintained separately and revised after Phase 2 screening. At M1, interviewers use the category framing in Section 8 to scope conversations; detailed probes will be distributed after hub co-design.

### Closing (All Categories)

1. Ask whether anything important was not covered
2. Confirm willingness to be re-contacted for fact-checking
3. Ask for referrals to other relevant stakeholders (with permission to name the referrer)
4. Confirm post-interview data handling (how notes or recordings will be stored)
5. Thank the participant
