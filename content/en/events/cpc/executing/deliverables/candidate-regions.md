# Candidate Regions and Phase 2 Screening Outcomes

During Phase 2 ([Milestone 2](/en/events/cpc/executing/fundamentals/milestones/milestone-2)), we screened the eight candidate regional clusters defined in Milestone 1 across all ten dimensions of the Regional Adoption Readiness Framework.

Three emerging-market regions anchored by our active [Communities of Place](/en/collaborators/communities-of-place/hub/hubs) (**Buenos Aires Hub**, **Lagos Hub** and **Nairobi Hub**, each working with three Local Project Teams, nine teams in total) advance to Phase 3 fieldwork (`42 to 51` planned interviews). Four regions (**Iberia / Spain**, **Brazil**, **the Andean countries** and **Mexico**) remain on desk monitoring (`0` Phase 3 interviews), and one region (**Horn / Ethiopia**) is excluded.

You can read the full scoring breakdown, weight sensitivity tests and infrastructure ratings in the [Market Prioritisation Matrix](/en/events/cpc/executing/deliverables/market-prioritisation-matrix), and inspect the specific workflows being tested in the [Use-Case Hypothesis Register](/en/events/cpc/executing/deliverables/use-case-hypothesis-register). East Asia is outside the scope of this contract.
