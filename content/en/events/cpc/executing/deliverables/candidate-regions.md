# Candidate Regions & Phase 2 Screening Outcomes

Country-cluster screening set evaluated across all ten dimensions of the Regional Adoption Readiness Framework (`RARF`) in Phase 2 (`Milestone 2`). Cities denote operational hub and access points inside each regional cluster. Detailed dimension scores, sensitivity models (`S0-S4`), and infrastructure prerequisite ratings: [Market Prioritisation Matrix](/en/events/cpc/executing/deliverables/market-prioritisation-matrix). Named plays to test in Phase 3: [Use-Case Hypothesis Register](/en/events/cpc/executing/deliverables/use-case-hypothesis-register).

East Asia is out of scope for this engagement.
