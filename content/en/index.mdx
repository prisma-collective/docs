Prisma services hub-networks via a temporary relationship to create temporary coherence and interpretation capacity. Networks could emerge at the continental level, as with [CATS](/en/events/summit), or at the regional level, as with [Argentina](/es/eventos/argentina). Every network is regionally grounded by the hubs. We don't try to mix across the networks, and we also don't become the networks.

We service the network with an event contract - a primitive - as a container in which network-scale organisational development can take place. We own that primitive. That's our responsibility. We don't own the event. The event is open, collaborative and participatory.

Some common use-cases of events are outlined at [prisma.events/use-cases](https://www.prisma.events/use-cases).
