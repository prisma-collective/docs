# Prisma DIDs

Cardano-native decentralized identifiers and verifiable credentials for event-scoped enrolment, authentication, and access management across the federated Prisma network.

**Current specification:** v1.6.2 (December 2025)

## Architecture at a glance

Prisma DIDs separates **global identity infrastructure** from **forkable credential infrastructure**:

| Layer | Components | Operator | Scope |
| ----- | ---------- | -------- | ----- |
| **Global** | DID Dashboard, DID Indexer | Prisma | All `did:cardano` identities |
| **Forkable** | VC Interface, VC Indexer | Per organization | Org-specific credential types and schemas |
| **On-chain** | CIP-20 metadata under L_DID (199674) and L_VC (199675) | Wallet holders | Immutable event log |
| **Off-chain** | IPFS (DID Documents), PostgreSQL (indexer state) | Pinning + indexer hosts | Resolution and status queries |

The [DID indexer](/processes/enrolment/dids/indexer-api) is the shared resolution layer: consumer apps call `GET /did/:did` rather than scanning the chain directly. Roles issued during [enrolment](/processes/enrolment) are reflected in DID Documents and consumed by downstream auth flows (see [Auth & Access Examples](/processes/enrolment/dids/auth-access-examples)).
