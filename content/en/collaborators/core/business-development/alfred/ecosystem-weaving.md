# Ecosystem Weaving

Director of Ecosystem Strategy, Partnerships & Growth: the longer-term direction of my contribution.

This page sits under [Alfred](/collaborators/core/business-development/alfred). The role described here is intended, not yet held. It is the direction I am developing toward through the checkpoints described there.

## Purpose of the Role

The purpose of this role, as I intend to hold it, is to steward the development of Prisma's wider ecosystem by weaving relationships across communities of place, communities of practice, partner Hubs, institutions, field-builders and Capital Partners, creating the enabling conditions for action-learning journeys to connect into longer-term transition pathways, resource flows and self-organising forms.

My focus is on what surrounds and follows an action-learning journey: the Networks that inform it, the partners who resource it, and the pathways along which what was enacted continues, owned by the place where it happened.

## Domains of Practice

### Ecosystem

I map and sense relationships across Hubs, Networks, partners and Capital Partners, and keep a useful picture of where potential, gaps and connections exist.

### Partnerships and resources

I develop relationships with partners, institutions, Capital Partners and technology providers, and help structure engagements around shared potential and sustainable resource flows.

### Hubs and Networks

I support Hubs and Networks to connect local context with wider relationships and resources, while they retain ownership of their own context, narrative and development.

### Growth

I work to grow the number and depth of action-learning journeys and engagements Prisma can sustain, with attention to learning, organisational development and longer-term value rather than activity volume alone.

### Organisation

I feed external learning and ecosystem signals into Prisma's wider development, and contribute to how the organisation evolves with the ecosystem around it.

## Responsibilities and Accountabilities

These are intended responsibilities, activated as the trajectory develops. My current interim responsibilities remain under Business Development.

- Stewarding relationships across partners, Capital Partners, Networks and Hubs.
- Developing opportunities and partnerships from first conversation into sustained engagement.
- Connecting Hubs, Networks and partners into relevant action-learning journeys.
- Carrying the Protocol's business model into external conversations and feeding relevant learning back into it.
- Carrying local context into wider conversations, and wider resources back toward place, without losing provenance.
- Supporting case-study publication and documentation of emerging patterns.

## Learning and Evolution

### Known Edges and Learning Questions

- How do I build partnerships in a way that remains accountable to the Hubs and places involved?
- How do I carry local context, in my case from Northern Nigeria, into wider partner conversations without flattening it?
- How do I move from Hub-level work to organisation-level contribution while remaining grounded in place?
- What evidence is useful for understanding ecosystem health at this stage?

### Review Process

The role and its scope are reviewed at each trajectory checkpoint with Tabs and the core team, and revised through practice and learning.
